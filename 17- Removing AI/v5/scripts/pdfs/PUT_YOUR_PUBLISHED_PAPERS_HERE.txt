DROP YOUR PUBLISHED PAPERS HERE
================================

Put your own published papers (PDF format) in this folder.
These are used to learn YOUR personal writing style.

Examples of what to put here:
  my_paper_2023.pdf
  journal_article_2022.pdf
  conference_paper_2024.pdf

The more papers you add, the better the style profile.
3-10 papers is ideal.

After adding papers, run:
  python run.py style

This will:
  1. Extract text from your PDFs
  2. Analyse your sentence patterns, transitions, and vocabulary
  3. Write your personal style profile to:
     references/my_writing_style.md

That profile is then used when checking your drafts — feedback
will say "different from YOUR writing" not "different from generic norms".
