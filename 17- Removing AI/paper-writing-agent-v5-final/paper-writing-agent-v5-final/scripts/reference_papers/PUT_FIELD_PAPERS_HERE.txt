DROP 2-3 TOP PAPERS FROM YOUR TARGET FIELD HERE
=================================================

Put recent highly-cited papers from the field/venue you are
targeting (PDF format) in this folder.

Examples:
  best_paper_acl_2024.pdf
  nature_ml_2023.pdf

These are used to extract current field vocabulary so your
draft uses the right technical terminology.

After adding papers, run:
  python run.py style

This writes references/domain_vocabulary.md with field-current terms.
