    Who are you?

    Your project

    Type it once. It is added to every prompt for you. Not sure yet? Leave it empty or use the sample.

    Copy the prompts into ChatGPT or Claude

    Run them in the same chat, one after another, so the AI remembers your work. Attach the files each prompt lists.

    Check the answer you got

    Paste the AI's whole answer here (use the chat's copy button). You'll see its score, its weak points, and a quick check of whether you can trust it. Nothing leaves your browser.

    Your score over rounds

    Each answer you paste that has a score is added here, so you can see your idea getting better round by round. Saved only in this browser.

    How to tell if the answer is good

    • Every paper and number can be checked. Click 2–3 links, open 2–3 result files. If something is made up, tell the AI and ask it to remove it.
    • Every score or judgement has a reason. A number with no reason is not useful.
    • It says what it could not check. An honest answer lists what it did not see or run.
    • It ends with one clear next action. That is what you do first.
